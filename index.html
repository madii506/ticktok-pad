Who holds the money?

Nobody here. pump.fun’s own fee sharing splits every creator fee, locked in the same signatures that create the coin. Before you sign, this page reads the transactions and stops if the split isn’t the one shown. Anyone can push waiting fees out to everyone with one click.

How are first viewers drawn?

The moment a coin is recorded, up to 8 $TICKTOK viewers are drawn, seeded by Solana’s latest finalized blockhash. Tickets: new 1x, regular 1.5x (day 3), fan 2x (day 10), day one 3x (day 30). Until anyone has joined, the viewers’ 15% stays with the launcher.

What happens if a viewer sells?

They stop watching. If a wallet’s $TICKTOK drops below what it held right after joining, it leaves the draw. Coins it was already a first viewer of keep paying it.

Can I post the toks on TikTok?

Yes. Every tok downloads as a real video, made right in your browser. Post it anywhere.

When does a coin go quiet or die?

Six hours without a trade and it goes quiet. A week without a trade and it’s dead. One trade brings it back.
